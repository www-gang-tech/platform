This note is the public form of a finding. The private lab note stays private. What survives review is this: off-centre placement on a pad without magnets is a worse coupling problem than a centred placement, and magnets make that centre placement repeatable. That is a physical observation we can describe without a product SKU. A bench supply reading from the same session is not a public finding, a Wireless Power Consortium certification, or a charger specification.

So the research record is: **alignment is in scope; unpublished power is not.** The [Qi2 Magnetic Power Profile](/research/qi2-magnetic-power-profile/) is the public standard we point to. The [charger](/objects/charger/) page stays in development until certified hardware exists. That is the whole publication rule for this class of finding: rewrite what a reader can check, leave the rest in the brain.
