The Wireless Power Consortium publishes Qi2, an update to the Qi wireless charging standard. Qi2 includes a Magnetic Power Profile (MPP): magnets are used so a transmitter and a receiver can sit in a defined alignment, rather than relying on chance placement on a pad. WPC maintains a public register of certified products. Certification, not marketing language, is the check for whether a given charger or phone implements a stated Qi2 profile.

This page does not list wattage for uncertified GANG hardware. Power levels and logos belong to certified products and to WPC’s current documentation. Further reading should start at the Wireless Power Consortium’s Qi2 material, not at secondary blog summaries.
