GANG–1 is designed to bring wireless charging onto the wall.

The current specification lists a 120 V input, a 160 W integrated power supply, and a 4 ft extension cord. Devices charge wirelessly; the hub still connects to power.

## Included for setup

- One 4 ft extension cord.
- Two US-compatible outlet plates.
- One custom screwdriver.
- Two drywall screws.
- Four square screws in various lengths.

These are the accessories listed in the specification sheet. Installation requirements and the complete setup guide will be published before orders open.

## See it in your space.

Think about where you reach for your devices, the space around the hub, and access to power.

The specification considers spaces from home offices and entry areas to barbershops, gyms, lounges, retail, and restaurants. [Contact us](/pages/contact/) about the setting you have in mind.
