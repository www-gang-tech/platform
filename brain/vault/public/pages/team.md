GANG is the public name of the studio. Legal correspondence uses Gang Tech, LLC, New York. Code that is meant to be public sits with [www-gang-tech](https://github.com/www-gang-tech). Press and general mail are on [Contact](/pages/contact/).

This page is not a set of invented biographies. When someone should be public, they get a heading, a role, and a sentence they have signed off — the same file-owned rule as every other page. Until then, the studio is the named author.

Work in public is the [charger](/objects/charger/) and the notes linked from it. How those files are edited is on [Studio](/studio/). Who the studio is, in one page, is [About](/about/).
