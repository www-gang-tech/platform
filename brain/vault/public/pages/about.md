GANG makes charging objects and publishes notes that can be checked against a public standard or a measured claim. The studio is small. The website is the public cut of the work: Markdown files in a repository, reviewed in git, built as static HTML. Saving a draft does not put a page on the internet.

This version is for reading and for a design review. It is not a product catalogue, a CMS demonstration, or a checkout. The [charger](/objects/charger/) is in development; wattage, compatibility, price, and ship dates are withheld until they can be supported. [Research](/research/) holds source-backed notes. [Journal](/journal/) holds practice and process.

How pages are written and released is on [Studio](/studio/). Who to write to is on [Contact](/pages/contact/). The studio is not listed as a marketing roster; see [Team](/team/).
