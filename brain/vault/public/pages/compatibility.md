The right fit starts with your device and case.

GANG–1 is being developed for magnetic wireless charging. The current specification lists the devices below. Check both wireless charging support and magnetic attachment for use on the vertical face.

## Devices in the specification

| Device family | Models listed | Case and placement notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apple iPhone | iPhone 15, 15 Plus, 15 Pro, 15 Pro Max; iPhone 16, 16 Plus, 16 Pro, 16 Pro Max | The sheet also lists MagSafe-enabled iPhones 12 and later. Check the exact model and case. |
| Samsung Galaxy | S25, S25+, S25 Ultra; S24 series | The sheet specifies compatibility through a Qi2-certified magnetic case. |
| Google Pixel | Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro; Pixel 9 series | Pixel 9 series requires a Qi2-certified magnetic case according to the sheet. |
| Other Android | Select OnePlus, Xiaomi, and Motorola edge+ models; Qi2.0+ devices | Individual models are not fully enumerated. Send us the exact model and case to check. |
| Accessories | Apple AirPods and Qi2/MagSafe accessories | Check the exact accessory or charging-case model and its magnetic attachment. |

Listed compatibility does not mean every device charges at the specified 25 W maximum. The product remains in development.

[Download the specification sheet (PDF, 638 KB)](/assets/documents/gang-specifications.pdf).

## Check the case

A case is part of the charging setup. Magnetic attachment and wireless charging support both need to be considered.

## Check the position

The sheet lists legacy Qi 1.0 charging with the pad flat. That does not establish attachment to the vertical face. For wall-mounted use, confirm the device or accessory can attach magnetically as well as charge wirelessly.

Wired device charging is not supported; the hub has no USB-C charging ports.
