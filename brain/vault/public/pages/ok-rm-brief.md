This brief is for a design review of GANG’s public website. It is a draft in the repository so it appears in local preview and stays out of the production build.

## What to look at

Home, Studio, About, Objects, Research, Journal, Team. The charger as an in-development object, not a shop listing. Three charging guides and two development notes in Journal. Related links, revision notes, and Now / Next / Learned where they appear. Team is a short true page, not a portrait grid.

## Constraints we are holding

Website copy lives in repository Markdown. Save does not deploy. Release is git review plus the existing production build. Private evidence is not linked from public pages. Technical claims are limited to public standards and clearly labelled development status.

## What we are not asking for

A CMS, a database of content, a public assistant, or checkout. Those are out of scope for this version.
