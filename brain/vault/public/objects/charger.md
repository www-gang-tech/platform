This charger is in development. It is intended as a magnetic charging object for everyday use. Finish, geometry, and electronics are not final. We will not yet publish output in watts, a list of compatible phones, a price, or a date; those wait on measurement and certification.

What we will publish is the alignment problem the object is trying to solve, pointers to the public Qi2 Magnetic Power Profile, and guides that stay at the level of practice rather than product specification. See [Qi2 Magnetic Power Profile](/research/qi2-magnetic-power-profile/) and [charging with magnets](/journal/magnetic-alignment/).
