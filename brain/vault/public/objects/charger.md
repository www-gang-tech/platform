## Questions, answered.

### How many charging zones does it have?

Four wireless charging zones in one hub, each specified for up to 25 W. Check the compatibility information for listed devices and case requirements.

### Can I charge by USB-C?

No. This product is designed for wireless charging and does not include USB-C charging ports.

### Is it completely wireless?

Devices charge wirelessly. The hub uses a 120 V input and an integrated 160 W power supply. A 4 ft extension cord is included.
