This site is a considered prototype for a design review. It is not a CMS product demo, a shop, or a public assistant. Home, Studio, About, Objects, Research, and Journal are the structure; copy is written to be read, not to exercise the platform. What stays out of this version: a new CMS, a content database, checkout, and automated publication. Files, git, and a static build remain the release path.
