Hotels and cars often offer a pad. Treat it as unknown hardware. If the phone sits off-centre or warms quickly, use a cable. A short certified cable still earns its place in a bag; magnets are for the desk you trust. Airline and hotel outlets vary. This page does not recommend a specific adapter family; follow the device maker and the venue. Related: [everyday charging](/journal/everyday-charging/).
