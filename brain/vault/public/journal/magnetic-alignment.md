A phone on a pad charges well when the coils overlap. Magnets make that overlap less of an accident. Qi2’s Magnetic Power Profile is the public name for that idea. If a charger and a phone both implement a magnetic profile, they should pull into a repeatable place. If only one side has magnets, treat alignment as a visual check: centred, still, and cool to the touch after a few minutes.

Do not assume a logo on a box matches the phone in your pocket. Look up the certified product, not the accessory photograph. Continue with [everyday charging](/journal/everyday-charging/) or the [Qi2 note](/research/qi2-magnetic-power-profile/).
