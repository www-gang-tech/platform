Keep the contact faces clean. Cases, coins, and cards in the way of a coil waste heat. If the phone gets hot, lift it off: heat is a signal, not a feature. Prefer one overnight location rather than a trail of pads. Magnets help at that location; they do not fix a cluttered desk.

When you need a number — watts, time to full — use the phone’s own reading and the charger’s certified listing. This guide does not invent either. See also [travel charging](/journal/travel-charging/).
